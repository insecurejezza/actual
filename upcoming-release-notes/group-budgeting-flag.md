Add an experimental group budgeting option that lets you budget a total straight to a category group, see how much of it is still to distribute, hand some of it out to a category, cover a group's overspending from it, move it to another group, or put it back into To Budget. Groups can also have their own budget automation, so a group's total is filled in for you each month. The budget month you get from the API reports each group's amount to distribute too.
